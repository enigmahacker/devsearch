# Code of Conduct

DevSearch aims to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to anyone affected, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/enigmahacker) or the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/enigmahacker/devsearch/security). Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially, and reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may act on any behavior they judge to break this code. Depending on how serious and repeated it is, that can mean:

1. **A private warning** explaining what happened and why it was a problem.
2. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
3. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations, including harassment.

Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code may be edited or removed.

DevSearch v1.0.5 is live 🚀

Stop juggling 12 tabs of docs, GitHub issues and Stack Overflow threads. DevSearch searches them together and gives you an AI answer where every claim cites its source.

What's in it:
✅ Bring your own AI key: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, DeepSeek or a local model
✅ Runs 100% on your machine. Your key never leaves your browser except with your question
✅ Filter by framework and version
✅ One command to install, one to update: ds update

Try it:
npm i -g @aryptn/devsearch && ds
